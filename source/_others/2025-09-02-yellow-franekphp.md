Known as _Le Monstre_, the FrankenPHP elePHPant was produced when the FrankenPHP
GitHub repo reached 10 000 stars.  It was available to attendees of various
conferences in France and is no longer available for distribution.

It is slightly larger than a typical elePHPant, features a faux stitched head,
tusks, and more.
