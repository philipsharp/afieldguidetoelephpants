First [teased](https://x.com/MonsieurBiz/status/1252673019983642632) in April
2020 this elephpant launched a [Kickstarter campaign](https://www.kickstarter.com/projects/734475887/sylius-elephpant/)
in December 2023 and reached its funding target a month later. Production began
in June 2024 and finished in late August.  When they finally arrived at the
Monsieur Biz offices in October the team discovered the factory produced the
elePHPants in blue instead of green.  The factory restarted the entire order and
15 months after the Kickstarter campaign launched the green‑colored elePHPants 
finally arrived.  Kickstarter backers recieved one blue elePHPant with their
order in addition to the green elePHPants they had ordered.
