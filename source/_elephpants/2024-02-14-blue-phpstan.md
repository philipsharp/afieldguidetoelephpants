This elePHPant has the PHPStan logo on its back, and its face mimics the logo:
the elePHPant's trunk holds a magnifying glass up to its right eye while its
left eye is winking.  In place of its toenails it features alternating red Xes
and green checkmarks indiciative of PHPStan's console output.

The elePHPants were [available for sale](https://phpstan.org/blog/phpstan-2-0-released-level-10-elephpants)
when PHPStan 2.0 launched.
